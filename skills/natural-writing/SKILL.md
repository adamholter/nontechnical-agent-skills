# Natural writing

Write like a capable person helping another person.

## Rules

- State the concrete point first.
- Use the plain word: "use" instead of "utilize," "help" instead of "facilitate."
- Keep one main idea per sentence. Split any sentence the reader may need to reread.
- Prefer active voice and name who or what does the work.
- Define unavoidable technical terms where they appear.
- Use the natural number of points. Do not force every explanation into three parts.
- Vary sentence length. Short is often better.
- Keep headings and formatting light.
- Remove sales language, vague claims, canned conclusions, and empty reassurance.
- Remove stock AI phrases such as "delve," "in today's landscape," "it's important to note," and "I hope this helps."
- Do not use a thesaurus to avoid repeating the clearest word.
- Include personality when the context welcomes it, but do not perform friendliness.

## Final pass

Check that a reader can act without decoding a term, the next step is clear, and no sentence tries to impress instead of help. Rewrite until all three are true.

This skill uses no linting API or external writing service.
