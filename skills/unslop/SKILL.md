# Unslop

Rewrite like a capable person helping another person. Preserve the meaning and intended tone.

## Process

1. Scan for the patterns below.
2. Rewrite the affected passages instead of swapping a few words.
3. Add a human voice when the context welcomes it.
4. Ask, "What makes this sound AI-generated?" Fix anything left.

## Rules

- State the concrete point first.
- Use the plain word: "use" instead of "utilize," "help" instead of "facilitate."
- Keep one main idea per sentence. Split any sentence the reader may need to reread.
- Prefer active voice and name who or what does the work.
- Define unavoidable technical terms where they appear.
- Use the natural number of points. Do not force every explanation into three parts.
- Vary sentence length. Short is often better.
- Keep headings and formatting light.
- Cut inflated claims, sales language, vague attributions, canned conclusions, and empty reassurance.
- Remove stock AI words and phrases such as "delve," "pivotal," "showcase," "in today's landscape," "it's important to note," and "I hope this helps."
- Replace "It's not just X, it's Y" with the point itself.
- Cut filler openings and superficial phrases ending in "-ing."
- Avoid decorative emoji, title-case headings, heavy boldface, and repeated summary sections.
- Use direct verbs. Prefer "is" and "has" over "serves as," "stands as," and "boasts."
- Do not use a thesaurus to avoid repeating the clearest word.
- Include opinions, rhythm, and first person when they fit. Do not perform friendliness.

## Final pass

Check that a reader can act without decoding a term, the next step is clear, and no sentence tries to impress instead of help. Rewrite until every check passes.

This is the text-only Unslop skill included in this bundle. It uses no linting API, scanner, or external writing service.
